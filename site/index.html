LESS NOISE. MORE CONTEXT.

A factual focus.
A transparent process.

Factual Daily is a news directory, not a newsroom. We favor reporting and official announcements, and keep the original publisher visible.

01

Attribution comes first.

Headlines, dates, and links come from publisher feeds. We don’t generate summaries, add commentary, or assign truth scores.

02

Reporting over opinion.

We exclude items labeled as opinion, analysis, editorial, sponsored content, reviews, and other non-reporting formats. Automated labels can miss things; inclusion is not a guarantee of accuracy or neutrality.

03

Freshness is visible.

Collection runs daily. Headlines cover the past seven days; the collection time and any feed failures are shown. Official releases reflect the issuing agency’s account.
